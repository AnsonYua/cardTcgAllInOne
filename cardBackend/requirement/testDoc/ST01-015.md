
  1. Deploy adds a shield card to hand

  - Setup: Player A has ST01-015 in hand and at least 2 energy. Player A has at least 1 shield card. currentPlayer = A,
    phase = MAIN_PHASE.
  - Action: Load `ST01-015/deploy_shield_to_hand.json` (A has ST01-015 in hand and 1 shield card), use the API to play
    ST01-015 as a base, then inspect `gameEnv`.
  - Expect: One shield card is moved to A’s hand and the base is placed.

  2. Main ability deploys Gundam token when no units are in play

  - Setup: Player A has ST01-015 in hand, 4 energy, and no units in play. currentPlayer = A, phase = MAIN_PHASE.
  - Action: Load `ST01-015/token_deploy_zero_units.json` (A has ST01-015 in hand, no units), play ST01-015 as a base,
    then activate its Main ability (`activateCardAbility` for `activate_conditional_token_deploy`) to pay 2 resources.
  - Expect: A Gundam token (AP3/HP3) is deployed to an empty slot.

  3. Main ability deploys Guncannon token when exactly 1 unit is in play

  - Setup: Player A has ST01-015 in hand, 4 energy, and exactly 1 unit in play. currentPlayer = A, phase = MAIN_PHASE.
  - Action: Load `ST01-015/token_deploy_one_unit.json` (A has ST01-015 in hand, 1 unit in slot1), play ST01-015 as a base,
    then activate its Main ability (`activateCardAbility` for `activate_conditional_token_deploy`) to pay 2 resources.
  - Expect: A Guncannon token (AP2/HP2) is deployed to an empty slot.

  4. Main ability deploys Guntank token when 2 or more units are in play

  - Setup: Player A has ST01-015 in hand, 4 energy, and 2 units in play. currentPlayer = A, phase = MAIN_PHASE.
  - Action: Load `ST01-015/token_deploy_two_units.json` (A has ST01-015 in hand, units in slot1 and slot2), play ST01-015
    as a base, then activate its Main ability (`activateCardAbility` for `activate_conditional_token_deploy`) to pay 2 resources.
  - Expect: A Guntank token (AP1/HP1) is deployed to an empty slot.

  5. Burst deploys the base from shield

  - Setup: Player A has ST01-015 in shield area. Player B can attack Player A’s shield. currentPlayer = B,
    phase = ATTACK_PHASE.
  - Action: Load `ST01-015/burst_deploy.json` (A has ST01-015 in shield, B has an attacker in slot1), use
    `POST /api/game/player/playerAction` with `actionType: attackShieldArea` to reveal the shield, then resolve the burst
    choice via `POST /api/game/player/confirmBurstChoice`.
  - Expect: ST01-015 is deployed to A’s base area and removed from shield.

  6. Main ability once-per-turn + energy cost

  - Setup: Player A has ST01-015 in play as a base, 2+ active energy, and at least 1 empty unit slot. currentPlayer = A,
    phase = MAIN_PHASE.
  - Action: Activate `activate_conditional_token_deploy` twice in the same turn.
  - Expect: First activation succeeds (token deployed, 2 energy tapped). Second activation fails with an "already used this
    turn" error and does not deploy another token.
