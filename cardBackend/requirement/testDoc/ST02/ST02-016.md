
  1. Deploy on your turn creates Tallgeese token

  - Setup: Player A has ST02-016 in hand, no ST02-016 in trash, at least 1 empty slot, and it is A's turn. currentPlayer = A, phase = MAIN_PHASE.
  - Action: Play ST02-016 as a base.
  - Expect: One Tallgeese (AP4/HP2) token is deployed to an empty slot.

  2. Deploy with Corsica Base in trash creates two Leo tokens instead

  - Setup: Player A has ST02-016 in hand and another ST02-016 in trash, with at least 2 empty slots. currentPlayer = A, phase = MAIN_PHASE.
  - Action: Play ST02-016 as a base.
  - Expect: Two Leo (AP1/HP1) tokens are deployed instead of Tallgeese.

  3. Burst deploy does not create tokens

  - Setup: Player A has ST02-016 in shield area and it is Player B's turn. currentPlayer = B, phase = MAIN_PHASE.
  - Action: Player B attacks shield area and ST02-016 bursts into play.
  - Expect: ST02-016 is deployed, but no tokens are created because it is not Player A's turn.
