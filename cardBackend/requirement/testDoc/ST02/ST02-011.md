
  1. Paired battle-destroy draw (your turn)

  - Setup: Player A has ST02-011 paired with a unit. Player A's deck has at least 1 card. Player B has a unit that can be destroyed. currentPlayer = A, phase = MAIN_PHASE.
  - Action: Player A attacks and destroys B's unit with the paired unit.
  - Expect: Player A draws 1 card (hand size +1) and a CARD_DRAWN notification is queued.

  2. No draw when not paired or not your turn

  - Setup: Same board, but ST02-011 is not paired or currentPlayer = B.
  - Action: Player A destroys a unit in battle.
  - Expect: No draw occurs.
