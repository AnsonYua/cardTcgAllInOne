
  1. Main ability applies AP-3 to a chosen enemy unit

  - Setup: Player A has ST01-014 in hand and 2 energy. Player B has a unit in slot1. currentPlayer = A,
    phase = MAIN_PHASE.
  - Action: Load `ST01-014/main_ap_reduction.json` (A has ST01-014 in hand; B has ST01-005 in slot1), use the API to play
    ST01-014 as a command, then activate its Main ability to choose the enemy unit.
  - Expect: The chosen enemy unit has `modifyAP: -3` (or total AP reduced by 3) for the turn.

  2. Action step ability applies AP-3 during battle action step

  - Setup: Player A has ST01-014 in hand and 2 energy. Player B has a unit in slot1. currentPlayer = A,
    phase = MAIN_PHASE.
  - Action: Load `ST01-014/action_step_ap_reduction.json` (A has ST01-014 in hand and a unit in slot1; B has a unit in
    slot1 and a blocker in slot2), use `POST /api/game/player/playerAction` with `actionType: attackUnit` to open
    ACTION_STEP, then play ST01-014 as a command and activate its Action ability to choose the enemy unit.
  - Expect: The chosen enemy unit has `modifyAP: -3` (or total AP reduced by 3) for the turn.

  3. Action step with blocker choice before action ability

  - Setup: Player A has a unit in slot1 to attack with and ST01-014 in hand. Player B has a target unit in slot1 and a
    blocker in slot2. currentPlayer = A, phase = MAIN_PHASE.
  - Action: Load `ST01-014/action_step_ap_reduction.json`, declare an attack on B slot1 with
    `POST /api/game/player/playerAction` (actionType: attackUnit).
  - Expect: A blocker selection flow is required and provides an `eventId`.
  - Action: Call `POST /api/game/player/confirmBlockerChoice` selecting the blocker, then play ST01-014 as a command and
    activate its Action ability to choose the enemy unit.
  - Expect: The chosen enemy unit has `modifyAP: -3` (or total AP reduced by 3) for the turn.

  4. Burst activates Main ability when revealed from shield

  - Setup: Player A has ST01-014 in shield area. Player B can attack Player A’s shield. currentPlayer = B,
    phase = ATTACK_PHASE.
  - Action: Load `ST01-014/burst_activate_main.json` (A has ST01-014 in shield, B has an attacker in slot1), use
    `POST /api/game/player/playerAction` with `actionType: attackShieldArea` to reveal the shield, then resolve the burst
    choice via `POST /api/game/player/confirmBurstChoice`.
  - Expect: ST01-014 activates its Main effect from burst; a target selection flow is required and the chosen enemy unit
    gets AP-3 for the turn.
