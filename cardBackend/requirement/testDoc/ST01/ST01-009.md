
  1. Blocker choice redirects an attack and rests the blocker

  - Setup: Player A has a unit in slot1 to attack with. Player B has ST01-009 in slot1 (active) and another unit in
    slot2 to be the original target. currentPlayer = A, phase = MAIN_PHASE.
  - Action: Load `ST01-009/blocker_choice_redirect.json` (A has ST01-005 in slot1; B has ST01-009 in slot1 and ST01-005
    in slot2), use `POST /api/game/player/playerAction` with `actionType: attackUnit` targeting B slot2.
  - Expect: The response indicates a blocker selection flow is required and provides an `eventId`.
  - Action: Call `POST /api/game/player/confirmBlockerChoice` selecting ST01-009 (`carduid`, `zone`, `playerId`), then
    inspect `gameEnv`.
  - Expect: ST01-009 becomes rested and the attack target is redirected to ST01-009 (current battle target matches the
    blocker carduid).

  2. ST01-009 cannot attack the enemy player

  - Setup: Player A has ST01-009 in slot1 and 2 energy. Player B has no units. currentPlayer = A, phase = MAIN_PHASE.
  - Action: Load `ST01-009/cannot_attack_player.json` (A has ST01-009 in slot1), use `POST /api/game/player/playerAction`
    with `actionType: attackShieldArea` using ST01-009 as the attacker.
  - Expect: The action fails with an error indicating the unit cannot attack the player, and no battle is started.
