
  1. Attack effect readies a rested resource when Suletta is paired and attacking

  - Setup: Player A has ST01-006 in slot1, ST01-011 (Suletta) in hand, and 2 energy with one rested. Player B has
    ST01-005 in slot1. currentPlayer = A, phase = MAIN_PHASE.
  - Action: Load `ST01-011/attack_activate_resource.json` (A has ST01-006 in slot1, Suletta in hand, one rested energy;
    B has ST01-005 in slot1), use the API to play Suletta as a pilot pairing with ST01-006.
  - Expect: Suletta is attached to ST01-006 and the rested energy remains rested before attacking.
  - Action: Use `POST /api/game/player/playerAction` with `actionType: attackUnit` from ST01-006 targeting B slot1, then
    inspect `gameEnv`.
  - Expect: One rested energy becomes active (isRested false).

  2. Attack effect does not trigger when a different unit attacks

  - Setup: Player A has ST01-006 in slot1, ST01-005 in slot2, Suletta in hand, and 2 energy with one rested. Player B has
    ST01-005 in slot1. currentPlayer = A, phase = MAIN_PHASE.
  - Action: Load `ST01-011/attack_no_trigger_other_attacker.json` (A has ST01-006 in slot1, ST01-005 in slot2, Suletta in
    hand; B has ST01-005 in slot1), use the API to play Suletta as a pilot pairing with ST01-006.
  - Expect: Suletta is attached to ST01-006 and the rested energy remains rested before attacking.
  - Action: Use `POST /api/game/player/playerAction` with `actionType: attackUnit` from ST01-005 (slot2) targeting B slot1,
    then inspect `gameEnv`.
  - Expect: No energy is readied (rested energy stays rested).

  3. Burst adds Suletta to hand when revealed from shield

  - Setup: Player A has ST01-011 in shield area. Player B can attack Player A’s shield. currentPlayer = B,
    phase = ATTACK_PHASE.
  - Action: Load `ST01-011/burst_add_to_hand.json` (A has ST01-011 in shield, B has an attacker in slot1), use
    `POST /api/game/player/playerAction` with `actionType: attackShieldArea` to reveal the shield, then resolve the burst
    choice via `POST /api/game/player/confirmBurstChoice`.
  - Expect: ST01-011 moves to A’s hand instead of trash and the shield count decreases by 1.

  4. Once-per-turn resets on the next turn

  - Setup: Player A has ST01-006 in slot1, Suletta in hand, and 2 energy with one rested. Player B has ST01-005 in slot1.
    currentPlayer = A, phase = MAIN_PHASE.
  - Action: Load `ST01-011/attack_activate_resource.json`, play Suletta as a pilot pairing with ST01-006, then attack
    with ST01-006.
  - Expect: One rested energy becomes active after the attack.
  - Action: Call `POST /api/game/player/endTurn` for Player A, advance to Player A’s next turn, then attack again with
    ST01-006.
  - Expect: The energy ready effect can trigger again on the new turn (once-per-turn resets).
