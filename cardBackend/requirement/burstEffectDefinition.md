 Burst Effect Definition

 When a card with Burst is revealed from a shield during an opponent’s shield attack, it would normally go to trash.
 Instead, the Burst effect triggers immediately at reveal time.

 For ST01-010 (Amuro Ray):
 - Trigger: Card is revealed from the shield area during a shield attack.
 - Effect: “Add this card to your hand.”
 - Resolution flow: reveal → Burst effect triggers → card moves to hand instead of trash.
